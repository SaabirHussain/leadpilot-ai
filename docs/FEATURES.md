# What is included

## Real features
- One local owner login, business profile, tone/signature and timezone settings.
- Manual leads, CSV validation/dry-run/import, search, filters, status and opt-out.
- Dashboard, reminder tracking, activity, follow-up schedules and contact history.
- Editable drafts, revisions, approval, copy, opening your email app and manual sent recording.
- OpenAI-compatible and Anthropic adapters using your own provider/model/key.
- Inbound server-to-server lead webhook with a generated bearer token.
- JSON/CSV exports, demo deletion, lead deletion and workspace reset.
- Responsive light/dark interface with locally bundled fonts; no CDN dependency.
- Docker packaging and prebuilt native-Python installation path.

## Demo-mode features
- Fictional Northstar Web Studio leads and business profile; all demo emails use example.com.
- Rotating simulated inquiries, dated activity, overdue/today/upcoming examples.
- Deterministic template drafts labeled **Demo draft**, without provider network calls.

## Not included in v1.0
- **Gmail sending or any automatic email transport.** Copy drafts or open your email app.
- Autonomous outreach, bulk sending, background AI generation or automatic scheduling of email sends.
- Gmail OAuth, inbox syncing, reply detection or delivery/open tracking.
- Hosted SaaS accounts, team roles, multi-business tenancy or cloud synchronization.
- Packaged n8n workflows, payment processing, guaranteed SEO/business outcomes.
- Guaranteed AI accuracy, prompt-injection immunity or regulatory compliance.

Follow-up dates are reminders, not automated sends. Record replies/status changes
yourself. AI warnings are limited pattern checks, not a full factual/safety review.
