# LeadPilot AI — film storyboard

All people/businesses are fictional. Real app footage uses template-based Demo
drafts, visibly labelled. No music, voice-over, sound effects or audio track is
included. Narration below is optional seller recording copy, not synthesized audio.

## Full film — 82.5 seconds

Individual takes total 85s. Five 0.5s crossfades shorten the assembled edit to 82.5s.
Times below are source-take timing; subsequent scenes overlap by 0.5s at each cut.

| Scene | Source time | Visuals | On-screen text |

## NARRATION_SCRIPT

143 words: roughly 70–80 seconds at a relaxed 110–125 words/minute, leaving
brief visual pauses. Optional narration only; rendered videos are silent.

> A new inquiry arrives. Then another. Between client work and everyday admin,
> keeping track of the next step can get complicated.
>
> Meet LeadPilot AI: a local workspace for organizing leads, preparing replies,
> and planning follow-ups.
>
> Here, a fictional catering inquiry appears in the demo workspace. Open the lead
> to see the customer's message, contact details, status, and follow-up timeline.
>
> Generate a first draft, choose a tone, and make the wording your own. This example
> uses the clearly labelled Demo draft mode. Check the details and next step before
> copying it to your email app.
>
> You review. You send. LeadPilot never sends email automatically.
>
> After sending yourself, record the contact to schedule the next follow-up. The
> reminders view helps you see what's overdue, due today, and coming up. Activity
> history keeps the context close.
>
> LeadPilot AI. Your leads, organized. One-time purchase. Download and install.
