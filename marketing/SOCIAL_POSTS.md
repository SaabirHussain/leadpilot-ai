# Social media copy (draft)

## LinkedIn launch post
I just released **LeadPilot AI**, a small follow-up kit for freelancers, web studios and local service businesses.

The problem it solves is simple: inquiries come in, a few get answered, and some quietly go cold.

LeadPilot keeps every lead in one dashboard, writes a personalized first draft from the customer's own message and your business profile, and schedules your next follow-up in your time zone. When someone replies, opts out, or you mark the deal won or lost, the reminders stop.

A few deliberate choices:
• It never sends email on its own. You review, edit and send every message.
• It runs on your own computer, and your lead data stays there.
• Use demo mode with no AI key, your own OpenAI/Anthropic key, or a free local model.
• One-time purchase. No subscription.

If you've ever found an unanswered inquiry two weeks too late, this is for you. Link in the comments.
